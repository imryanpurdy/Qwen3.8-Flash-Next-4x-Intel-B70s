## Why AWQ + the flag

1. **Accuracy:** devan's checkpoint misreads long-context values at 6/24 (cold), 4/24 (warm) at 118K.
   AWQ: 0/24, 1/24 flagless; 1/24, 2/24 with the flag (within run-to-run noise of flagless — one answer each).
2. **Stability:** without the flag, AWQ froze 3× under prefix-cold recall load (00:33, 00:58, 02:06 UTC Oct 4):
   all 4 TP workers stuck in `async_tensor_h2d` H2D copy (py-spy), engine core blocked in `shm_broadcast.wait`.
   dmesg: `xe ... Engine reset: engine_class=ccs|bcs` bursts at every freeze — the **bcs (copy) engines** were resetting.
3. **The fix:** `UR_L0_V2_FORCE_DISABLE_COPY_OFFLOAD=1` (from vllm#41663, same DEVICE_LOST class on B70) disables the
   Level-Zero v2 copy-offload path. 60-min soak (8×concurrent, mixed 60K/118K depths, 3632/3632 OK): **zero engine
   resets**, zero restarts, p50 7.7 s. Speed 49.3 tok/s (−0.4% vs devan's 49.2).
